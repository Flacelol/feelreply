Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Information we collect

We collect your email address, business name, and Google Maps listing URL when you create an account. We also store the reviews and AI-generated replies associated with your account.

2. How we use your information

Your information is used solely to provide the FeelReply service: detecting reviews, generating AI replies, and sending email notifications to you. We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Email notifications

We send email notifications to the address you provide when new reviews are detected. You can disable notifications at any time from your dashboard settings.

4. Payment information

Payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store your credit card details. Stripe's privacy policy applies to all payment data.

5. Data storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, hosted on AWS infrastructure in the EU region. Data is encrypted at rest and in transit.

6. Data retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by emailing feelreply@gmail.com.

7. Cookies

We use only essential cookies required for authentication. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

8. Contact

For privacy-related requests, contact us at feelreply@gmail.com.
